## Capture and release checks

- Use the actual Lens Studio Preview and mouse-driven simulated pinch; keep the circuit and control feedback readable at the intended viewpoint.
- Narrate in English or provide an English translation. Hide unrelated private editor/account content without obscuring the interaction being demonstrated.
- Record the final project revision; use permitted assets and audio. Background music is unnecessary.
- Confirm the final duration is below 3:00, the running Lens appears in the first ten seconds, and the continuous interaction block remains intact after editing.
- Upload to YouTube with **Public** visibility. Check playback while signed out; verify the submitted description and repository match the footage. No video URL exists yet.
